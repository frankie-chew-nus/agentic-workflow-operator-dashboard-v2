## 1. Current project logic

A — **Research & Source Qualification**  
What potentially useful knowledge exists?

B — **Evidence Synthesis & Strategy Formulation**  
Turn approved evidence into candidate hypotheses / starting directions.

C — **Local Integration & Stage 1 Backtesting**  
Build, integrate, optimise and backtest candidates. C is a search/experimentation environment; it does **not** have to reproduce B exactly. The fixed evaluation conditions must not be moved by the optimisation process.

D — **Stage 2 Walk-Forward / OOS Validation**  
Freeze a surviving C candidate and test it against evidence it was not optimised against.

E — **Commissioning Audit & Decision**  
Human Operator decides whether the evidence is sufficient for deployment.

Working shorthand: **B generates hypotheses → C searches → D falsifies → E decides.**

## 2. Endpoint / non-negotiable gates carried from Gate 1

The deployment decision remains conditional on:
- CPC index >= 1.2 after mandatory 9-pip transaction friction.
- Maximum drawdown < 15%.
- Broker/server activity < 2,000 daily activities.
- Zero Martingale/grid structures; averaging-down is also treated as prohibited in the W5 working design.
- Zero third-party black-box dependencies.
- OOS/walk-forward evidence, with >=100 OOS trades in the Gate 1 proof standard.
- Final commissioning remains Human Operator owned.

Do not silently change these thresholds during optimisation or validation.
