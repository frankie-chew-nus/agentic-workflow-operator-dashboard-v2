Charter box
**Line that held: Charter Line 3 — Accountability & Boundary.**  
**Event that tested it:** W7 integration removed routine Human approvals at M2 and duplicate Human review at M7, while retaining Human authority only where judgement or capital accountability remains: evidence admission, exception resolution, search expansion, integrity review and final commissioning. The boundary held; Human ownership does not mean approving every step.
